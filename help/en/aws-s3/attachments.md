# S3

Administration › AWS › **S3** shows how Redmine keeps attachments in Amazon S3: the **Environment variables** that turn the plugin on (with the private key shown only as "Set" or "Not set"), the **Connection tests** of reading and writing the bucket and of delivery through CloudFront, the **Files blocked by malware scan**, the **Consistency check** between the attachments and the bucket, and the **Settings** of the signed URLs and the local cache.

More: [Redmine AWS S3: administration page](https://redmine-kit.github.io/redmine_aws_s3/admin.html) and [attachments](https://redmine-kit.github.io/redmine_aws_s3/attachments.html).
